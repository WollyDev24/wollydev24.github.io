Why another DSBmobile app?

The official DSBmobile app hasn’t received an update in over two years and isn’t native at all. DSBmaterial is a from-scratch, open-source rebuild that loads your school’s substitution plan with a modern Material 3 Expressive interface — including dynamic color from your wallpaper.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don’t

A focused set of features built around the Material You design language.



Material You Expressive

Cutting-edge Material 3 Expressive components — rounded 28dp surfaces, smooth motion, and the Google Sans Flex type system.



Dynamic Color

On Android 12+ the app builds its whole color scheme from your wallpaper, or pick one of 7 curated seed palettes.



Local Webserver

Host the current plan as a single self-contained page on your local network — open it in any browser on any device, no app needed.



Home Screen Widget

A glanceable widget keeps the plan visible right on your home screen, matching your theme.



Auto Refresh

Background syncing refreshes the plan automatically, so the latest substitutions are always there when you open the app.



Archive & Calendar

Scroll the archive of past days or browse the whole plan in a calendar view, and share any day as a beautiful card.



German Localization

Fully localized in German, with correct time and date formatting — right at home in a German school day.



Free & Open Source

No ads, no tracking, no account. Apache-2.0 licensed and built with contributions in mind.
